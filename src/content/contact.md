# CONTACT

## BUGS AND FEATURE REQUESTS

Open an issue at
[github.com/gildrb/eukhe/issues](https://github.com/gildrb/eukhe/issues).
Include your eukhe version, your platform, and what you ran. Issues are public: leave secrets, tokens and private paths out
of them.

## SECURITY

Report a security problem privately: email
[mail@gildrb.com](mailto:mail@gildrb.com) with "eukhe security" in the
subject, or open a private security advisory on the GitHub repository.
Please do not open a public issue for it.
